Keep your ember burning.

Ember helps you build habits one day at a time. Set a goal like “21 days of exercise”, check it off every day and watch your streak grow under its own sky.

• Goals from 7 to 365 days, with ready-made templates and symbols
• Streaks, best streak, progress and a celebration when you finish
• A gentle daily reminder, only on days you haven't checked off yet
• Home screen widgets with your goal and a daily quote
• Available in 29 languages
