Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Ember is a motivation and habit tracking app. It has no user accounts and no server of its own. The goals you create and the days you check off stay on your phone. This policy explains what the app uses, what leaves your phone, and the choices you have.

1. Data that stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your goals, their names, symbols, durations and themes
  • The days you mark as done, streaks and progress
  • Reminder time, language and other preferences
  • Data shown in Ember's home screen widgets (shared only between the app and its widgets on your phone)

We cannot see, access or restore this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ember is free and is funded by banner ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interaction and app usage data, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information

On iOS you are asked whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised. In the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and in US states where it is required, a Google consent form asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown.

Your goals and check-ins are never shared with advertisers.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. “Remove Ads” purchase

The one-time “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Apple (App Store). We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store on your phone whether the purchase is active; no purchase data is sent to us or to any other service.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your phone and are only shown if a goal has not been checked off that day. No push notification server is used.

5. Children

Ember is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Turn reminders off in the app or in iOS notification settings.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app.

Because we do not hold your personal data, we cannot export or delete it on our side. For requests about data held by Google, use their tools linked above or write to us and we will help.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
